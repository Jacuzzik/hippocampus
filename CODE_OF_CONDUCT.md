# Code of conduct

Hippocampus is a small open-source project. We want it to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and send changes without being treated badly.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Keep to the subject: the code, the design, the bug.
- Say what you think plainly, and accept that others may disagree.
- Give credit, and thank people who give their time.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, or posting other people's keys or secrets.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately the way [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes (a private advisory on the project's GitHub page), and say it is a conduct report. We read every report, keep it private, and answer.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or ban people who do not follow this page.
