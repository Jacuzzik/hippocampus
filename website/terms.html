    Terms

    Terms

    Hippocampus is free and open source under the MIT licence. These terms cover the website and the optional account. Using the app on your own computer needs no account and no agreement beyond the licence.

    The app

    The app is given as it is, without a warranty, under the MIT licence in the repository. Your notes are yours and stay on your computer. You are responsible for the agents you add and for what they do with your files, within the limits you set in Settings.

    The account

    • An account is optional. It is made by signing in with Google, and you can delete it at any time in the app or on the account page; that deletes everything stored for it.
    • The free plan has limits so the service stays up for everyone: 20 live share links, 50 new links a day, and a size cap on shared notes and on synced settings.
    • Do not use share links to spread material you have no right to share, to harm others, or to attack the service. A link locks after 10 wrong passwords.
    • An account used for abuse can be suspended. The app keeps working without it.

    Your content

    A note you share stays yours. The link lets whoever has the password read it until it expires, and you can delete the link earlier. We keep nothing else of it.

    Changes and contact

    If these terms change, the change is listed in the changelog. To report a security problem, use the private report on GitHub; for anything else, open a feedback issue. How data is handled is on the privacy page.

    Hippocampus

    Your notes, a graph of them, and agents that work in them. Free and open source. This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
