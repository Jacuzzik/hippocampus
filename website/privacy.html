Privacy

Hippocampus works fully on your own computer. It has no account, no telemetry and no server of its own.

What is stored, and where

  • Your notes stay in the vault folder you chose.
  • A search index of the notes is kept in the app's data folder. It is a cache; deleting it loses nothing.
  • API keys, if you add any, go to your system keychain, never to a file.
  • An error log is kept on your computer. Keys are masked in it. Settings, Privacy and data shows and clears it.

What leaves your computer

  • What an agent sends to its own provider (for example Anthropic for Claude Code), under that provider's terms.
  • Nothing else. The network switch in the Control panel can stop model calls altogether.

This website

It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no files from other sites.
