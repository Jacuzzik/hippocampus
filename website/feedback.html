Feedback

Tell us what works, what does not, or what you would like the app to do. A short form on GitHub asks four questions.

Open the feedback form

What helps

  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The app version, from Settings > About.
  • A screenshot, or a few lines of the log from Settings > Advanced.

The form needs a free GitHub account. Remove anything private from logs and screenshots first: the form is public.

Security problems

Do not use the form. Follow the security page to report it privately.
