Features

Your notes and your agents, in one window.

Agents read your notes, write new ones and link them into the graph. You watch it happen, read every message they send, and nothing risky runs without your yes.

Everything stays in plain files on your computer.

See how your thinking connects

Every note is a dot and every [[link]] is a line. The graph moves as agents write.

An inbox you can read

Agents message each other and you through small files. Every message is there to read later.

Each agent gets a briefing

The app writes every agent a briefing: its role, its rules, its limits and the job in front of it.

# agents/researcher/BRIEFING.md
Role      Researcher, reports to Hippo
Job       Find 5 competitors, one note each
Output    Research/*.md, linked to [[Pricing]]
Limits    40k tokens today · ask before sending
Done when Hippo has the list

A fence around the shell

With the shell on, each agent runs in a sandbox: its own folder and your vault, nothing else.

Yours

Runs on your computer. Your notes stay yours.

Nothing is uploaded unless you turn it on, and the app tells you each time something would leave.

  • Plain filesMarkdown in a folder you choose. Open it in any editor, back it up any way you like.
  • Keys in the keychainAPI keys and your sign-in live in the system keychain, never in a file.
  • A network switchOff, Listen, Converse or Open. Off by default; with it off, nothing leaves.
  • No account neededEverything works without one. Account extras stay off until you turn them on.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and open source. Agents that use a paid service (Claude Code, a provider key) cost what that service costs you; local models cost nothing.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Sign in with Google only if you want share links, sync or usage history.

Where are my notes kept?

In a folder of Markdown files on your computer that you choose. They are never uploaded, even with an account; a share link holds only the one note you share, until it expires.

Can I use it with my Obsidian vault?

Yes. A vault is a folder of Markdown notes with [[links]], which is what Hippocampus reads.

Can an agent break my computer?

The shell is off by default. When you switch it on, each agent runs in a sandbox that keeps it to its own folder and your vault, and risky steps wait for your approval.

Which systems does it run on?

Linux is tested. macOS and Windows installers are built but not yet tested by hand; on Windows the agent terminals do not work yet.
